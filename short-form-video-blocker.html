Start free

Still Free removes the YouTube Shorts shelf and tab, and opens Shorts links in the normal video player. It needs no account, and the blocking settings stay on your device.

What Still Pro adds

Still Pro costs $1.99 once, with no subscription. It removes Instagram and Facebook Reels, blocks the TikTok website, and syncs Still settings. Buy on any supported surface, then sign in with the same email to unlock Pro on every supported surface.

Private by design

Still runs only on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. Free blocking happens on your device. Still has no ads, behavioral tracking, or browsing-history analytics. Pro sign-in stores only the account, purchase entitlement, and settings needed to restore your purchase and sync your settings.

Native mobile apps are different

Still cannot remove Shorts or Reels inside the native YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok apps on iPhone and iPad. That is a browser-extension limit, not a setting you missed. Read why, and see what Still can change in Safari.
