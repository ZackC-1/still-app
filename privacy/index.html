Still — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Still is made by Cadmus Labs LLC. This policy covers our apps, browser extensions, website and support service.

Still 2.0 removes YouTube Shorts and Instagram/Facebook Reels and blocks the TikTok website for free on supported web surfaces. Blocking needs no account or purchase. Optional sign-in enables free settings sync. On iPhone and iPad, Still works in Safari websites, not native social-media apps. See 2.0 release availability before installing.

Browsing and local settings

Still does not collect your browsing history or the content of pages you visit. Access to the supported websites lets Still remove or block the features you select. Your blocking settings can stay on your device, and blocking works without signing in.

Using Still without an account does not mean no information reaches service providers. The Apple app connects to RevenueCat before sign-in, and online requests involve connection information.

Information we use and service providers

  • Account and settings sync: Supabase processes your email address, account ID, authentication information and synced Still settings when you choose to sign in. Settings describe your choices, not a history of websites you visit.
  • Sign-in email: Resend processes the recipient address, message content and delivery information needed to send sign-in emails. Open and click tracking are disabled for our sign-in email domain. Delivery and security records still exist.
  • Service security: Supabase processes request information such as IP addresses, timestamps and outcomes, along with our temporary security counters, to operate and protect account access and backend services.
  • App identity and historical purchases: RevenueCat maintains app-user identities, purchase history and entitlement information. The Apple app starts with a generated anonymous app-user ID. Signing in associates RevenueCat with your Still account ID, which can be linked to your account. RevenueCat also records connections and customer country information and provides purchase analytics. Apple holds underlying App Store purchase records. No purchase is needed for Still 2.0.
  • Support correspondence: our support, privacy and general-contact addresses forward to a Google Workspace mailbox. We receive your address, message and any attachments so we can respond.
  • Website visits: GitHub Pages hosts our website and records visitor IP addresses for security. This is separate from the websites you use Still to block. See GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub's privacy statement.

RevenueCat documents that customer country can come from a transaction or an IP-derived country, and that it discards the IP used for that derivation. This does not mean all services involved in Still keep no IP logs. See RevenueCat's customer documentation.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising tracking. We do not collect browsing analytics. RevenueCat's purchase analytics are separate from browsing activity. Its only configured integration for Still sends events to our Supabase backend.

Providers may process information outside your country. For example, Resend documents that it stores customer email data in the United States; selecting an email sending region does not change that storage location. See Resend's processing information.

How long information remains

Local settings and account safety

Settings remain on your device while you keep the local app or extension data. A minimal last-synced-account marker remains after sign-out or account deletion to prevent settings from one account being copied into another on a shared device. It is not an IP address or browsing history.

Account and synced settings

We keep your active account and synced settings until you delete the account. The deletion process and its limits are described below.

Temporary security counters

Our counter database stores a count, a short time window and a derived identifier for an account, email address or internet connection. It does not store raw IP addresses. Connection identifiers change between windows and are not linked to an account history. Deriving an identifier is a safeguard; it does not necessarily make the record anonymous.

Counters use one-minute or ten-minute windows. With cleanup operating normally, expired counters and their window-specific keys are removed by the end of the window plus up to one minute and five seconds: at most eleven minutes and five seconds in total. An outage can delay cleanup. These periods describe active counter records, not provider logs or backup copies.

Service logs and database audit records

Separate service logs can contain IP addresses and request information. Our sign-in verification audit messages contain a time and outcome without recording the IP address. Earlier versions recorded IP addresses; deploying the change did not erase existing logs.

Supabase's Pro plan advertises seven-day log retention. That does not establish the lifetime of separate audit records stored inside the database or guarantee erasure of every underlying copy. Database audit records and separately stored billing events are not covered by the temporary-counter cleanup schedule. See Supabase's plan details and audit storage documentation.

Database backups

Supabase keeps daily physical database backups for recovery. Our plan provides a seven-day backup history; point-in-time recovery is disabled. Deleting a live record does not selectively erase it from an existing backup. Backup availability is not a guarantee that every underlying copy is erased at that exact deadline. See Supabase's backup documentation.

Sign-in email

Resend documents 30-day retention for email and log data on our Free plan and separate seven-day backups. Deleting your Still account does not immediately delete those records. Earlier removal of a specific message can be requested through Resend. These periods do not describe copies in your email account or our support mailbox. See Resend's retention information.

RevenueCat and purchase records

Deleting a Still account does not automatically delete its RevenueCat customer identity, aliases, historical purchases or separate backend billing-event records. Our current account-deletion flow sets no automatic expiry for those records. They support retained app identity and historical purchase services. You can contact us about removal. RevenueCat provides a separate customer-deletion operation; it does not erase Apple's underlying store records. See RevenueCat's customer-deletion documentation.

Support correspondence

Messages in our Google Workspace mailbox remain until we manually delete them. We do not currently apply an automatic deletion schedule. Deleting a Still account does not delete that correspondence. You can request its deletion by contacting us. Deleting a mailbox message is not an immediate erasure guarantee for every provider copy. Google describes Trash, administrator recovery and any applicable Vault retention separately in its email recovery documentation.

Your choices and account deletion

You can use blocking without an account, stop settings sync by signing out, and request a copy of your account data by contacting privacy@stillapp.fit.

Choose Delete account in the app's signed-in screen to remove the authentication account, synced settings, account entitlement record and account-linked security counters from our active application database. Temporary connection counters may finish their existing short security window so deleting an account does not reset protection for other people using the same connection.

Local settings and the account-safety marker can remain on your device. Provider logs, database audit records, backups, email correspondence, RevenueCat history and underlying store records have the separate handling described above. Account deletion does not make all these records disappear immediately or guarantee that historical purchases transfer to a new account.

Contact privacy@stillapp.fit to request access, correction or deletion beyond the in-app process. We may need to verify that the request concerns your information. We will explain any records that cannot be removed through that request and the applicable limitations.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Still's use and transfer of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Still uses and transfers user data only to provide or improve its disclosed single purpose and user-facing features.

Children's privacy

Still is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so we can review its removal.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, we will update its date. For privacy questions or data requests, email privacy@stillapp.fit.
