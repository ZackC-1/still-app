Set up Still

Still removes YouTube Shorts and Instagram and Facebook Reels and blocks the TikTok website on every supported surface, free and with no account. Signing in is optional and free, and syncs your settings.

Still Pro, an optional one-time purchase, hides more: related videos, end-of-video suggestions, comments and live chat on YouTube (and stops autoplay); Explore, Stories, suggested accounts and Threads links on Instagram; Stories, the Video tab and sidebar ads on Facebook. Some extras work only on a computer.

  • iPhone and iPad: Safari
  • Mac: Safari
  • Chrome on a computer
  • Firefox on a computer

On iPhone and iPad, Still works on websites in Safari. It doesn’t work inside the YouTube, Instagram, Facebook or TikTok apps, or in Chrome or Firefox on a phone.

iPhone and iPad: Safari

  1. Install Still from the App Store and open it.
  2. On Turn on Still in Safari, tap Open Settings.
  3. Turn on Allow Extension, then allow Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok. If Settings opens on Still’s own page instead, go back, then go to Apps → Safari → Extensions → Still.
  4. Close Safari, then reopen it.
  5. Back in Still, tap I’ve turned it on.

To open Still in Safari on iPhone, tap the button at the left of the address bar, then tap “Still”. You can also change your settings in the Still app.

Setting up Still on your iPhone doesn’t set it up on your iPad. Do these steps on each device.

Mac: Safari

  1. Install Still from the Mac App Store and open it.
  2. On Turn on Still in Safari, click Open Safari Settings.
  3. In Extensions, check the box next to Still.
  4. In the Permissions section, allow Still on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok.
  5. Quit Safari (⌘Q) and reopen it.

To open Still in Safari, click the Still button to the right of the address bar. If the toolbar is narrow, it may be under ».

Chrome on a computer

  1. Open Still in the Chrome Web Store. Click Add to Chrome, then Add extension.
  2. On the setup page that opens, click Allow and confirm.
  3. Pin Still to your toolbar, as the setup page shows.
  4. Click Still in the toolbar. All four websites are on; turn off any you don’t want.

Install Still in each Chrome profile you use. You don’t need an Apple device.

Firefox on a computer

  1. Open Still on Firefox Add-ons. Click Add to Firefox, then confirm.
  2. On the setup page that opens, click Allow and confirm.
  3. Pin Still to your toolbar, as the setup page shows.
  4. Click Still in the toolbar. All four websites are on; turn off any you don’t want.

You don’t need an Apple device.

Check that it works

Reload any of these websites that were already open, then:

  • YouTube: Shorts are gone. A Shorts link opens as a normal video.
  • Instagram and Facebook: Reels are gone. A Reel someone sends you still plays, but the next Reel and the Reels feed don’t.
  • TikTok: the website shows Still’s blocking page.

Optional: sync your settings

  1. Choose Sign in in the Still app, or in Still’s popup or settings in Chrome or Firefox.
  2. Use the same email address on each device. Enter the code we email you. If it doesn’t arrive, check Spam or Junk.

Sync carries your settings only. You still need to install and turn on Still on each device and browser.

Optional: Still Pro

Choose Get Still Pro in the Still app or in Still’s settings in Chrome or Firefox. In Chrome and Firefox, you sign in first. The extras start off; turn on the ones you want. Already bought it? Choose Restore purchase.

If it’s not working

  • Check you’re in the browser and profile where you installed Still.
  • Check Still is turned on and allowed on the website, then reload the page. In Safari, open the Still app; if it shows Turn on Still in Safari, follow its steps.
  • For private browsing, allow Still in private windows in your browser’s extension settings.
