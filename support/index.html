Still Support

Still removes YouTube Shorts and Instagram and Facebook Reels and blocks the TikTok website on every supported surface, free and with no account. Optional free sign-in syncs your settings. Still Pro, an optional one-time purchase, hides more on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook, such as comments, Stories and recommended videos.

Contact us

Email support@stillapp.fit. Tell us your device and browser, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot helps. Never send sign-in codes or card details.

Still isn’t blocking

Follow the setup guide for your device. Make sure Still is turned on and allowed on the website, then reload the page. In Safari, open the Still app; if it shows Turn on Still in Safari, follow its steps.

On iPhone and iPad, Still works on websites in Safari. It doesn’t work inside the YouTube, Instagram, Facebook or TikTok apps.

My sign-in code didn’t arrive

Check Spam or Junk, make sure the email address is right, then ask for a new code. Still nothing? Email us.

Still Pro and refunds

  • Don’t see Still Pro? Choose Restore purchase in the Still app, or in Still’s settings in Chrome or Firefox while signed in with the email you used to buy it.
  • App Store purchases: Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Chrome and Firefox purchases: email support@stillapp.fit from the address you used at checkout within 7 days of buying for a full refund, no questions asked. After 7 days, refunds are at our discretion. This doesn’t limit your rights under the law.

Your account and privacy

To delete your account, choose Delete account in Still while signed in. Blocking keeps working without an account. See the privacy policy or email privacy@stillapp.fit.
