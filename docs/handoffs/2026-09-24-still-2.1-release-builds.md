## Blockers and human gates

- **Signing:**
  - The Mac provisioning profile "Mac Team Provisioning Profile: com.chartash.still" lacks the iCloud
    capability and the `com.apple.developer.ubiquity-kvstore-identifier` entitlement.
  - This Mac has only an "Apple Development" signing identity. App Store export needs Apple
    Distribution signing: either Xcode automatic signing (cloud-managed certificates) or
    `-allowProvisioningUpdates` with an App Store Connect API key.
  - Both change the Apple Developer account and need the owner's approval.
- **iOS archive script:** `apps/apple/scripts/archive.sh` needs `ASC_KEY_ID`, `ASC_ISSUER_ID` and
  `ASC_KEY_PATH`. The alternative is Xcode Organizer, signed in as the owner.
- **Store uploads, submissions and privacy declarations:** owner actions.
