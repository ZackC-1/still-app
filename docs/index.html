Set up Still. Get on with your day.

On mobile

iPhone & iPad

  1. Download Still from the App Store.
  2. Open the app and follow the steps to enable Still in Safari.
  3. Browse in Safari. Still blocks short-form distractions automatically.

Works on websites in Safari, not inside social-media apps. Android isn’t supported.

On desktop

Chrome, Safari and Firefox

  1. Install Still for your browser: the Mac app for Safari, or the extension for Chrome or Firefox.
  2. Enable Still and allow website access. On Safari, follow the setup steps in the Mac app.
  3. Browse as usual. Open Still from the app or browser toolbar to choose what to block.

Install Still separately in each browser you use.

Want your settings to sync?

Open Still on each device or browser. Sign in with the same email and enter the code we send you. Your blocking settings sync automatically.
