Still supports Safari, Chrome, and Firefox.

Still keeps the useful parts of the websites you opened while removing the short-form routes that turn a quick visit into a long scroll.

Optional settings sync

Sign in with the same email on your supported browsers and devices to sync your Still settings for free. Blocking continues to work without sign-in.

Private by design

Still runs only on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. Free blocking happens on your device. Still has no ads and never records the pages or videos you visit. Usage data that helps improve the app can be turned off in settings. Optional sign-in enables settings sync. See our privacy policy for account and service-provider details.

Native mobile apps are different

Still cannot remove Shorts or Reels inside the native YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok apps on iPhone and iPad. That is a browser-extension limit, not a setting you missed. Read why, and see what Still can change in Safari.
