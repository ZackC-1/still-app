Still Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Still is made by Cadmus Labs LLC. This policy covers the Still apps, browser extensions, website and support.

Still removes YouTube Shorts and Instagram and Facebook Reels and blocks the TikTok website on every supported surface, free and with no account. Optional free sign-in syncs your settings. Still Pro, an optional one-time purchase, hides more on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook, such as comments, Stories and recommended videos.

The short version

  • Still doesn’t collect your browsing history.
  • Blocking works without an account.
  • We don’t sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • You can turn off usage data and delete your account at any time.

What Still sees on websites

Still only has access to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, so it can remove or block what you choose there. Blocking runs on your device. Still never records which sites, pages, videos, accounts or searches you view, or when you visit a website.

What we collect

If you sign in

We keep your email address, your account and your synced Still settings so your settings follow you between devices. Settings are your choices, not a record of the sites you visit. Our email delivery service sends your sign-in codes, with open and click tracking turned off.

If you buy Still Pro

In the iPhone and Mac apps you buy through Apple, which keeps the purchase under your Apple Account. In Chrome and Firefox you buy through a payment processor’s checkout, which collects your email address and payment details. Your card details never reach Still.

We keep only what we need to give you Still Pro and handle refunds and payment problems: that your account holds Still Pro, the product, the payment status and the transaction references from Apple or the payment processor. The iPhone and Mac apps check purchases with our purchase service using a random ID, even if you never sign in or buy anything. If you sign in, that ID is linked to your account. Our purchase service also records your country and connection details and gives us purchase statistics.

Usage data, while “Share usage data” is on

Our analytics service receives events about how you use Still itself: installs and updates, which store you got Still from, opening Still, turning switches on or off, setup and sign-in steps, the days you use Still and verified Still Pro purchases and refunds. Events carry random identifiers created on each device, the Still version and the kind of device and browser.

Usage data never includes the sites, pages, videos or searches you view, or your card details or receipt numbers. We ask our analytics service not to work out your location, and it discards IP addresses. If you sign in, our server links each device’s usage data to your account and adds your email address to that record. Your device never sends your email address to our analytics service.

Older versions of the Still apps and extensions kept an anonymous identifier in your iCloud or browser sync storage and linked usage data to your account ID when you signed in. They also sent one last event when you turned sharing off.

Our analytics service uses AI tools from AI companies it works with to help us find problems in usage data, errors and our public bug reports. Those companies may not use the data to train their models, though our analytics service can’t yet guarantee they keep no copy.

Support emails and our website

If you email us, we receive your address, your message and any attachments so we can reply. Our website’s hosting provider records visitors’ IP addresses for security.

Connection information

Requests to our servers include your IP address and request details, which our backend provider logs to run and protect the service. Our short-lived security counters use a derived identifier, never your raw IP address.

Firefox permissions

In Firefox, signing in uses Firefox’s authentication information permission, which you accept when you install Still. Firefox asks separately, when you first need it, before Still sends your email address, purchase information or usage data.

How we use it

We use this information only to run Still, sync your settings, provide Still Pro, answer your messages and improve Still. We don’t sell your personal information, use it for advertising or share usage data with advertisers or data brokers. Our providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States.

Still’s use and transfer of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep it

  • Settings on your device: until you remove Still’s app or extension data. A small marker of the last signed-in account stays after you sign out, so one account’s settings aren’t copied into another.
  • Account and synced settings: until you delete your account.
  • Service logs and backups: about seven days. Sign-in audit records in our database have no fixed expiry; they record a time and outcome, not your IP address.
  • Security counters: deleted within about 11 minutes.
  • Sign-in emails: kept by our email delivery service for 30 days, plus seven days of backups.
  • Usage data: one year. Records that connect a purchase to usage data expire after 90 days.
  • Purchase records: no automatic expiry, so we can handle refunds, disputes and restoring Still Pro. Apple and the payment processor keep their own records. Contact us about removal.
  • Support emails: until we delete them. You can ask us to delete yours.

Deleting your account

In Still, while signed in, choose Delete account. This deletes your sign-in account, synced settings, your account’s Still Pro record and security counters linked to your account. It also asks our analytics service to delete the usage data linked to your account, which it processes in weekly batches.

Deleting your account ends Still Pro access linked to it, and the purchase may not carry over to a new account. We keep the purchase references needed to handle later refunds or disputes without the link to your account; this doesn’t make them anonymous. Logs, backups and emails expire on the schedule above. Settings on your device stay until you remove Still. Usage data from a device that was never signed in isn’t linked to an account; email us to have it removed.

Your choices

  • Use blocking without an account, or sign out to stop syncing.
  • Turn off Share usage data in Still’s settings. It starts on in the iPhone and Mac apps and in Chrome; the Safari extension follows the Still app. In Firefox it stays off unless you allow Firefox’s “technical and interaction data” permission. Turning it off stops new events and discards any waiting on your device. It doesn’t delete events already sent. Turning it back on gives the device a new random identifier.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion at privacy@stillapp.fit. We may need to confirm the request is about you.

Children

Still isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. Contact us if you think a child has given us personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: privacy@stillapp.fit. Other help: support@stillapp.fit.
