Still — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Still removes short-form video (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook Reels) from the sites you visit. This policy explains what data Still collects, why, and your choices.

Short version: Still does not track you, show ads, or sell your data. The only data we handle is what's needed to (a) sign you in and (b) sync your settings and restore Still Pro across your devices — and only if you choose to sign in.

What we collect

Still works fully without an account — if you never sign in, Still keeps your settings on your device only and we collect nothing.

If you create an account to sync settings across devices, we process:

DataWhyWhere it's stored
Email addressAccount identity (you sign in with a 6-digit code sent by email)Supabase (our backend)
Account IDLinks your devices to the same account so settings syncSupabase
Your Still settingsThe features you've turned on/off and per-site pauses, so they match on every deviceSupabase
Purchase recordTo unlock Still Pro (a one-time purchase) and restore it on supported devicesApple or Stripe, and RevenueCat

We do not collect: browsing history, the content of pages you visit, location, contacts, device identifiers for tracking, or any analytics/advertising data.

Service providers

We share the minimum data above only with the providers that make the app work:

  • Apple — in-app purchases made in the iPhone, iPad, or Mac app (StoreKit).
  • Stripe — web purchases started from the Chrome or Firefox extension.
  • Supabase — stores your account and synced settings.
  • RevenueCat — processes and verifies your in-app purchase.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone else, and none of this is used for advertising or tracking.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Still's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account — In the app's signed-in screen, choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, settings, and purchase record from sync. (Your purchase still exists with Apple and can be restored if you make a new account.)
  • Export your data — You can request a copy of your account data.
  • Use Still without an account — Just don't sign in; everything stays on your device.

Children's privacy

Still is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.
