# Launch content pack — organic discovery pages

This is the editorial source for the four high-intent guides identified in the marketing playbook.
They are implemented as static pages in `docs/` and added to the sitemap:

- `block-youtube-shorts.html`
- `remove-instagram-reels-safari.html`
- `short-form-video-blocker.html`
- `browser-extensions-native-iphone-apps.html`

Do not publish a store CTA until that store listing is publicly available. The Chrome CTA is live;
the Safari and Firefox guides intentionally use Support rather than guessing a pending store URL.
Each guide repeats the Safari-only/native-app limitation where relevant and links only to live
listings.

## 1. How to block YouTube Shorts without blocking YouTube

**Title:** How to block YouTube Shorts without blocking YouTube

**Description:** Remove YouTube Shorts from a supported browser while keeping normal videos,
subscriptions, search, and the rest of YouTube available.

**Outline:**

1. Explain the problem: the useful YouTube visit versus the Shorts feed.
2. State the outcome: Still removes the Shorts shelf and tab and opens Shorts links in the normal
   video player; normal YouTube remains available.
3. Give browser-specific install and enable steps, linking only to live Chrome, Firefox, or App
   Store listings.
4. Add the troubleshooting sequence: extension enabled, YouTube allowed in Safari, Still/YouTube
   on, then reload.
5. Close with: YouTube Shorts blocking is free and needs no account. Still Pro adds Reels, TikTok,
   and settings sync.

## 2. How to remove Instagram Reels in Safari

**Title:** How to remove Instagram Reels in Safari on iPhone, iPad, and Mac

**Description:** Use Still Pro in Safari to remove Instagram Reels while keeping normal Instagram
posts, messages, and pages available.

**Outline:**

1. State the boundary upfront: this applies to instagram.com in Safari, not the Instagram iPhone or
   iPad app.
2. Explain that the user enables Still in Safari's Extensions settings and grants instagram.com
   permission.
3. Explain that Reels removal is included with Still Pro; use the same email after purchase to
   unlock supported devices.
4. Give the reload/troubleshooting sequence.
5. Link to the live App Store listing and Support page only after approval.

## 3. A short-form video blocker for Safari, Chrome, and Firefox

**Title:** A short-form video blocker for Safari, Chrome, and Firefox

**Description:** Still removes YouTube Shorts free and adds Reels, TikTok, and settings sync with
one Still Pro purchase across supported browsers and devices.

**Outline:**

1. Lead with the table of supported browser/device combinations.
2. Clearly separate Free (YouTube Shorts, no account) from Pro (Instagram/Facebook Reels, TikTok
   website block, sync).
3. Explain the same-email restore path and the precise phrase “every supported surface.”
4. Include privacy proof: four-site permissions, on-device blocking, no browsing-history analytics.
5. Include the native-mobile-app limitation in a distinct callout, then list live store buttons.

## 4. Why browser extensions cannot block native iPhone apps

**Title:** Why a browser extension cannot block YouTube Shorts or Instagram Reels inside iPhone apps

**Description:** Safari extensions work on websites opened in Safari; they cannot change YouTube,
Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok's native iPhone and iPad apps.

**Outline:**

1. Give the direct answer in the first paragraph.
2. Explain the distinction between a website in Safari and a native app without implying that Still
   is broken.
3. Say what Still does support: YouTube Shorts free in Safari; Still Pro adds Reels and the TikTok
   website block.
4. Provide exact enable steps for iOS 18+ and iOS 17 or earlier.
5. Link to Support and the relevant live listing; do not use this page to promise an app-blocking
   feature that does not exist.
