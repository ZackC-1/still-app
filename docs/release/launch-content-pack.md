# Launch content pack — organic discovery pages

Current editorial reference for the free Still release; reviewed September 14, 2026. The four
implemented guide sources live in `docs/` and publish separately through `gh-pages`. Use
[the marketing playbook](marketing-playbook.md), current [product specification](../PRODUCT.md)
and the owner's approved homepage copy. This file is guidance, not a command to republish pages.

## Shared claims

All four blocking services and optional sync are free. Blocking needs no account or purchase;
sign-in with the same email enables sync across separate supported installations. Safari on
Mac/iPhone/iPad and Chrome/Firefox on desktop are supported. Native social apps, mobile
Chrome/Firefox and Android are outside this release. Preserve normal YouTube/Instagram/Facebook
content; the entire TikTok website is blocked. Never promise permanent free pricing.

The owner approved direct App Store/Chrome/Firefox links and versionless homepage branding,
removed homepage rollout notices, and will wait for store rollout before broad promotion.
Do not restore those notices or overwrite the approved homepage text during a documentation audit.
Verify public listings before campaigns; retain the actual scope on secondary guides.

| Guide source | Intended reader outcome and content |
|---|---|
| `block-youtube-shorts.html` | Explain Shorts removal and normal-player redirection; show browser-specific setup and troubleshooting while preserving regular YouTube. All four services, not just YouTube, are free. |
| `remove-instagram-reels-safari.html` | Explain free Reels removal on instagram.com in Safari, website permissions and reload/troubleshooting; distinguish native Instagram. No Pro purchase step. |
| `short-form-video-blocker.html` | Compare supported browser/device combinations, free local blocking and optional free same-account settings sync. No free/paid feature split or restore requirement. |
| `browser-extensions-native-iphone-apps.html` | Explain why Safari extensions affect websites rather than native apps; link setup and accurate store destinations without promising a future native-app blocker. |
